CorpoRite Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

CorpoRite is a Chrome extension and web service that rewrites Hinglish or informal Indian English into professional corporate English. This policy covers the extension and the CorpoRite website.

CorpoRite runs only when you request a suggestion (for example, by pressing Alt+Shift+C or enabling optional auto-suggest).

Information we collect

  • Google account info — name, email, and profile picture when you sign in.
  • Text you submit for rewriting — sent to our servers only when you request a suggestion.
  • Usage data — credit balance and rewrite counts for billing and account management.
  • Extension settings — formality, tone, and site preferences stored in Chrome sync storage.
  • Payment info — processed by Stripe; we do not store card details.

How your information is used

  • To generate rewrite suggestions using AI on our servers.
  • To manage your account and credit balance.
  • To process credit purchases via Stripe.
  • To replace text in the field when you click Accept.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where data is stored

  • CorpoRite servers — account, credits, and rewrite requests.
  • Chrome local storage — your extension auth token (not synced).
  • Chrome sync storage — extension preferences (formality, sites, etc.).

Third-party services

We use the following third parties:

  • Google — sign-in authentication.
  • OpenAI — text rewriting (we send your text on your behalf; you do not need an API key).
  • Stripe — payment processing for credit purchases.

Permissions explained

  • storage — saves settings and auth token.
  • identity — Google sign-in for the extension.
  • Host access to CorpoRite servers — rewrite API and account management.
  • Content scripts — detect text fields and show the suggestion panel where you type.

Your choices

  • Disable the extension from the toolbar popup.
  • Sign out to remove your extension auth token.
  • Uninstall the extension to stop all data access.
  • Use manual shortcut mode (default) to save credits.
