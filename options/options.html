How to use

  1. Sign in with Google and connect your account.
  2. Open a website (WhatsApp Web, Gmail, LinkedIn, etc.) and click a text field.
  3. Press Alt+Shift+C for suggestions (or enable auto-suggest while typing).
  4. Click Accept or press Ctrl+Enter to replace your text.
  5. Select text first to rewrite only the selection.

Customize the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
